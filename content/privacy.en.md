## Operator

www.vtes.cz is run on a non-commercial basis for the Czech Vampire: the Eternal Struggle community by
Petr Muller (the "operator"), who is the data controller under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Contact for questions and requests about personal data: [spambox@afri.cz](mailto:spambox@afri.cz)

## What data we process

**Web server logs.** On every visit, the web server automatically records technical details of the request:
IP address, date and time, the page requested, the server's response, the referring page and the browser
identification (user agent). These logs are used only to keep the site running and secure and to fix
technical problems. The legal basis is the operator's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are
kept for about 10 days and then deleted automatically. They are not shared with anyone and are not used
to identify visitors.

**Cookies and tracking.** The site uses no cookies and no analytics, advertising or other tracking tools.

The site collects no other personal data; there is no registration, no forms and no comments.

## Third-party content

**YouTube video.** The tutorial video on the home page is loaded from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com,
operated by Google Ireland Limited) only after you click play. Nothing is loaded from YouTube before
that. Once you start playback, Google processes data about you under its
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); data may be transferred to the USA.

**Links to other sites.** The site links to other websites (e.g. Discord, Facebook, V:EKN, Archon).
Their operators are responsible for how those sites handle your data.

## Your rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, its correction or erasure, restriction of
processing, and to object to processing. Send requests to the contact above.

If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Czech
[Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ)](https://uoou.gov.cz/en/), or with the supervisory authority
in your EU country of residence.

*Effective from 24 November 2026.*
